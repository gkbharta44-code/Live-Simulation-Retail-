The perpetual order loop

Delper runs as a closed loop, from lead generation to doorstep delivery and back again. Each completed, protected order brings the buyer back for the next enquiry. The loop runs continuously, advancing a step every few seconds and returning to step 1 after step 8. Select any step to jump to it.

Before the loop runs live: Axis Bank (escrow), SGS (3PQC) and ONDC (buyer channel) are not yet signed. The 15-day acceptance window, QC cost and delivery cost are planning assumptions to replace with pilot data.

The loop only turns when the escrow release happens. Revenue is earned only on completed sales, so any shortfall in acceptance, repeat orders or QC pass-rate integrity slows every later step.
